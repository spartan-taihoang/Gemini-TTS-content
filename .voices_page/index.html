Worth a second look

Meditations use two different voices.

Four meditations use Olin, while Laying Down the Day uses Charon and runs faster (115 vs 93 wpm). If the meditations belong to one collection, one voice would keep them consistent.

Koda is lower than Jori.

Maya Brooks is introduced as a woman ("past her bedtime"), but Koda measures about 136 Hz, well below Jori. Listen to Maya's sample to confirm the voice suits the character.

How this was measured

Pitch (F0): the median fundamental frequency over voiced 40 ms frames, by autocorrelation on the sample clips on this page. Lower numbers mean a deeper voice. It is an estimate; listening is the final check.

Pace: words in the JSON divided by the length of the whole MP3, pauses included. A lower number means slower reading or more pausing.

Voice descriptions: based on these measurements and the style fields in the JSON, not on listening.
