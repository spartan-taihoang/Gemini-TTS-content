Backgrounds and sounds

Behind the voice: the soft pad (daily prayers at -9 dB, some meditations), harmony and a soft piano (meditations at -10 to -14 dB). The pad and piano are generated by scripts in the repo; harmony was made seamless from a supplied recording.

Why these can ship in a paid app. Each Freesound page above states the sound is released under Creative Commons 0: the author has waived their copyright as far as the law allows. Freesound's summary for each says you may copy, modify, distribute and perform the sound, even for commercial purposes, without asking the author's permission. That covers bundling a sound in a subscription app sold on the App Store, editing it (trimming, looping, levelling, as done here), and playing it as a standalone track.

No credit is required under CC0. Crediting the author anyway ("Sound by kijjaz on Freesound") is a courtesy, not an obligation.

What still matters. CC0 is the uploader's own declaration; Freesound moderates uploads, but the waiver is only as good as the uploader's right to give it. Keep, for every file, the page link, a screenshot showing the CC0 license and the download date. Not every Freesound sound is CC0: some require attribution (CC BY) and some forbid commercial use (CC BY-NC), so check the license on each page before using a new sound. This is general information, not legal advice.

Worth a second look

Koda plays both Maya Brooks and Jason Greene.

The two guests are in different series, but anyone who hears both episodes may notice they share a voice.

Cruz is both the narrator and a guest.

Listeners of the daily prayers and Cruz meditations will hear the same voice as Cruz Castle in Comfort in Trials 6.

Long pauses after Scripture.

The <long pause> tag is the one most often read aloud. Daily prayers no longer use it at all; dropping it can shorten the pause after a verse to under a second.

How this was measured

Pitch (F0): the median fundamental frequency over voiced 40 ms frames, by autocorrelation on the sample clips (or on the guest's own lines). Lower numbers mean a deeper voice. It is an estimate; listening is the final check.

Pace: words in the JSON divided by the length of the whole MP3, pauses included.

Voice descriptions: based on these measurements and the style fields in the JSON, not on listening.
