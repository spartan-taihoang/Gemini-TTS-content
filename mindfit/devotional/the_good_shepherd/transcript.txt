Welcome, and good morning. Today's devotional is The Good Shepherd... a slow walk through the twenty-third Psalm. You may know these words by heart. Let's walk through them as if for the first time.
The psalm is David's, and David knew sheep. Before he was a king, he was the youngest son, the one sent out to mind the flock. He knew how easily sheep wander, and how much they need someone to look after them. So when he looks for a picture of God, he chooses the work he knows... and he puts himself among the sheep.
The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters. He restoreth my soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name’s sake.
The Lord is my shepherd. Not only a shepherd. My shepherd.
Look at who does the work in these lines. He makes me lie down. He leads. He restores. The sheep's part is to stay near and to follow. Before you lead anything today... a meeting, a family, a classroom... you are first someone who is being led.
Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort me.
Did you hear the change? In the green pastures, David talks about God. He leads me. But in the valley, he begins to talk to God. Thou art with me.
The good shepherd doesn't promise a road without valleys. He promises His company on it. He carries a rod to protect, and a staff to guide and to draw us back. If today's path runs through a dark place, it does not mean you have lost the shepherd.
Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over. Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever.
The picture opens out, from a field to a table. The sheep has become a guest. The trouble is still in the room, and yet there is a meal, and a full cup. And behind you, following all the way, come goodness and mercy.
So think about the day in front of you. Where do you need to be led? Where do you need company, more than answers? You don't have to see the whole path this morning. You only need to know whose voice you are following.
Let's pray. Lord, my Shepherd, go ahead of me today. Slow me down where I am rushing, stay close where I am afraid, and bring me back when I wander. Open my eyes to Your goodness along the way. Amen.
Carry one line with you today... the Lord is my shepherd. When the day gets loud, come back to that one word, my. Go in peace, and go well led.
