For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the Lord, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end.
Jeremiah, chapter twenty-nine, verse eleven.
Lord, You know every thought about my future, and Your heart toward me is peace, not harm.
You have a purpose and a hope prepared for me.
As I sleep, I let go of my worries and trust Your good plans.
Amen.
