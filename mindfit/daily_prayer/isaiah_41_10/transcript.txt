Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.
Isaiah, chapter forty-one, verse ten.
Father, when fear whispers tonight, remind me that You are with me.
You are my God, and Your right hand holds me steady.
Thank You for Your strength when mine runs out, for Your help when I cannot manage alone.
I belong to You.
Amen.
