Yea, the darkness hideth not from thee; but the night shineth as the day: the darkness and the light are both alike to thee.
Psalm one hundred thirty-nine, verse twelve.
God, the darkness does not hide me from You.
Night and day are the same in Your sight.
As I lie down in the dark, I know You see me clearly and You are near.
I am not alone.
Amen.
