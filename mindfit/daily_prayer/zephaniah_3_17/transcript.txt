The Lord thy God in the midst of thee is mighty; he will save, he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love, he will joy over thee with singing.
Zephaniah, chapter three, verse seventeen.
Mighty God, You are here with me, closer than my own breath.
You delight in me and quiet me with Your love.
The thought that You rejoice over me with singing fills my heart with wonder.
I rest in Your joyful presence tonight.
Amen.
