And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.
Romans, chapter eight, verse twenty-eight.
Father, even the hard and confusing parts of my life are not beyond Your reach.
You work all things together for good because I am Yours.
I may not see the pattern yet, but I trust Your loving purpose as I close my eyes tonight.
Amen.
