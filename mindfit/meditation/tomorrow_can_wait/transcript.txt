Let your body settle into the quiet of this moment.
Feel the weight of your head on the pillow... your shoulders releasing into rest.
There is nothing left to do tonight.
Take a slow breath in... and let it go.
Again, breathing in... and breathing out.
Let your breathing find its own gentle rhythm.
Tomorrow is waiting... and that is all it needs to do.
The tasks you did not finish.
The words you did not say.
The plans you have not made.
They are still there, and they will be there in the morning.
Tonight, you are released from them.
Your mind may want to remind you of what is coming.
It may list the things you need to remember... the problems you need to solve.
Notice those thoughts... and let them drift past like clouds.
You do not need to hold them right now.
Breathe in peace... breathe out the weight of tomorrow.
Jesus spoke to people who carried the burden of tomorrow on their backs every single day.
He knew how the mind turns over worries in the dark.
And he said this.
Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself.
Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.
Matthew, chapter six, verse thirty-four.
Tomorrow has enough to carry on its own.
It does not need you to carry it tonight.
Let your breath be a simple prayer... a quiet yes to this present rest.
In and out... held by something larger than your worry.
Your body knows how to rest, even when your mind does not.
Let your body lead you now.
Softening... settling... releasing.
Tomorrow will come with its own grace, its own provision.
Tonight, you are safe.
You are held.
And you are loved, just as you are... in this quiet, unfinished moment.
Goodnight.
