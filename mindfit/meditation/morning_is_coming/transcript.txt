Let this be a quiet moment.
The day is done.
Whatever it held... it is finished now.
You can let your shoulders drop.
Let your hands rest open, and still.
Feel the weight of your body, held safely where you lie.
There is nothing more for you to carry tonight.
Breathe in slowly... and out, a little longer.
Let each breath be soft, and easy.
Some days are heavy.
Some days ask too much of us.
There are moments when weariness runs deeper than the body... when discouragement feels like a blanket you cannot throw off.
Tonight, if that is where you are... you are not alone.
God sees the weight you have carried.
He knows the hours that felt too long... the words that hurt... the disappointments that piled up like stones.
And even now... he is near.
Take another slow breath in.
And release it fully.
This day will not last forever.
Darkness does not have the final word.
There is a promise woven into the rhythm of time itself.
Night falls, yes.
But morning always comes.
The psalmist knew this.
He had seen sorrow... and he had seen the faithfulness of God meet him there.
And so he wrote these words.
For his anger endureth but a moment; in his favour is life: weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning.
Psalm thirty, verse five.
Weeping may endure for a night... but joy comes in the morning.
Not because we have solved everything.
Not because the trouble has vanished.
But because God's mercy is new every day.
Because he does not leave us in the dark.
Let your breathing slow even more now.
Picture the sky before dawn.
Still, and quiet... but not empty.
The light is already on its way.
You do not have to manufacture hope tonight.
You do not have to fix what is broken before you sleep.
You only have to rest... trusting that God is faithful.
He holds tomorrow.
He holds you.
And when the morning comes... his mercies will come with it.
So close your eyes... and breathe.
Let the silence settle around you, like a refuge.
The Lord bless you, and keep you.
The Lord make his face shine upon you, and be gracious to you.
The Lord lift up his countenance upon you... and give you peace.
Goodnight.
