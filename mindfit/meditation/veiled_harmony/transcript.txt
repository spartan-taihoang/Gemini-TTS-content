Let your shoulders drop.
Let your jaw go slack.
You have carried enough today.
Breathe in slowly through your nose.
Breathe out through your mouth... letting the day fall away.
Again, breathe in... and out.
There is a veil between you and all that is broken.
Not a curtain you have hung yourself, straining to hold things back.
But a veil God has placed there... tender, and firm.
Behind it, he is working.
The fractures you cannot fix.
The wounds you cannot reach.
The sorrows too deep for words.
He tends them all... while you sleep.
You do not need to see his hands at work.
You do not need to understand the method.
He asks only that you rest.
Breathe in once more, slowly.
Breathe out... releasing what you cannot mend.
This is not surrender to despair.
This is trust.
The God who knit you together in secret... knows every thread that has come loose.
He does not sleep.
He does not grow weary.
And while you lie here in the dark... he is faithful.
Scripture tells us this.
He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds.
Psalm one hundred forty-seven, verse three.
He does not wait for you to be strong enough.
He does not require you to earn his care.
He heals because it is his nature.
And he binds up... because he loves.
Let your breathing settle into its own rhythm now.
Let your body sink a little deeper.
The veil is still there.
And behind it, quiet work continues.
Stitches you cannot see.
Healing you cannot measure.
Grace that does not announce itself.
You are held.
You are tended.
And you are loved.
Rest now in that veiled harmony... where God works, and you are free to sleep.
May he give you peace through the night.
Goodnight.
