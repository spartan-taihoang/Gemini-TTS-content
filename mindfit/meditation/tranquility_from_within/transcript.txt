Tonight, we turn our hearts toward a peace that comes from beyond ourselves.
The world offers many things it calls peace.
A quiet house.
A finished task.
A resolved conflict.
These are good things... but they are fragile things.
They can be disturbed by a single phone call, a sleepless night, a change in circumstance.
But there is another peace... a peace that runs deeper than any trouble.
Breathe in that truth.
Breathe out anything that feels uncertain, or unsettled.
This peace is not something you create, or maintain through your own effort.
It is a gift, freely given.
Christ offers it to all who come to him.
It does not depend on your circumstances being just right.
It does not waver when life feels hard.
The peace he gives is rooted in who he is... not in what is happening around you.
Let your breathing continue... slow, and steady.
As you breathe, imagine laying down the weight of the day.
The conversations that did not go as planned.
The tasks left undone.
The worry about tomorrow.
You do not need to carry these things into your sleep.
Jesus spoke to his disciples on the night before his death.
He knew what was coming... the betrayal, and suffering, that awaited him.
And in that moment... he spoke words of peace.
Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you.

And the world cannot take it away... because it did not come from the world in the first place.
