You are held.
Not by your own strength... but by hands that do not tire.

When the weight of responsibility sits heavy on your shoulders.
When you wonder if you can bear it all.

God does not ask you to carry what he is already carrying.
He does not stand at a distance, watching to see if you will manage.
He is close... and he is strong.
Think of a child learning to walk, reaching up to hold a parent's hand.
The child grips tightly, thinking her small fingers are keeping her upright.
But it is the parent who holds... who steadies... who bears the true weight.
The child is safe not because of her grip... but because of the hand that will not let go.
Let your breathing slow, and deepen.

Trying to hold onto your plans, your people, your sense of control.

God spoke through the prophet Isaiah to a people who were afraid.
They had every reason to fear, by any earthly measure.
And into their fear, he spoke these words.
Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.
Isaiah, chapter forty-one, verse ten.
Fear not, for I am with you.
Not because the danger has passed.
Not because you have found your footing.
But because he is with you.
He will strengthen you.
He will help you.
He will uphold you with his righteous right hand.
That word, uphold.
It means to support... to sustain... to keep from falling.
Not to prop you up until you can stand on your own... but to bear you continually.
Breathe in that promise.
His hand is underneath you now, as you lie here in the dark.
It was underneath you today, when you felt unsteady.
It will be underneath you tomorrow.
You do not have to be strong enough.
You do not have to have it all figured out.
You are not held by your own competence, or your own faith, or your own effort.
You are held by him.
Let your shoulders drop, away from your ears.

There is nothing you need to do right now.
No problem you need to solve in this moment.
You can rest in his hand.
The same hand that formed the mountains and set the stars in place... is gentle enough to hold you.
Strong enough that nothing can pull you from his grasp.
Close your eyes, if they are not already closed.
Feel yourself sinking deeper into rest.
Tomorrow will come with its own needs and tasks.
But tonight... you are simply held.
The God who does not slumber or sleep is watching over you.
You can let go.
May you sleep in the safety of his presence.
May you rest in the strength of his hand.
And may you wake knowing that what holds you tonight will hold you still.
