Find a comfortable place to rest tonight.
Let your body settle into stillness.
Notice your breath... moving in, and out, without effort.
Each breath you take is a gift.
You do not make the air.
You do not create the rhythm.
You simply receive.
Breathe in slowly... filling your lungs with God's provision.
Breathe out... releasing the day.
Your breath connects you to the One who formed you.
It is ordinary, and miraculous, at once.
Let yourself breathe a little slower now.
There is no hurry.
There is nowhere else to be.
With each breath... you are held in God's care.
Scripture says this clearly.
The Spirit of God hath made me, and the breath of the Almighty hath given me life.
Job, chapter thirty-three, verse four.
The same Spirit that gives life... remains with you now.
As you breathe tonight, you are sustained by the breath of God.
Let your breathing be your prayer.
No words are needed.
Just this gentle receiving... again, and again.
Rest in the presence of the One who breathed life into you... and keeps you still.
May you sleep in peace... held by the God who gives every breath.
Goodnight.
