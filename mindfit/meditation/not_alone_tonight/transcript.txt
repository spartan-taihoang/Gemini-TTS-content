Maybe you feel the loneliness of the hour... the weight of being by yourself.
But here is the truth that meets you tonight.
You are not alone.
The Lord is with you in this room, at this moment.
He knows your name... and he knows this night.
He sees you.
Take another slow breath.
In the darkness, his presence is real.
Not an idea, or a memory... but a living presence.
Closer than the air you breathe.
The God who made the stars is here with you now.
He does not hurry.
He does not turn away.
He stays.
Breathe in his nearness.
Breathe out the loneliness.
Scripture tells us something beautiful about this.
And the Lord, he it is that doth go before thee; he will be with thee, he will not fail thee, neither forsake thee: fear not, neither be dismayed.
Deuteronomy, chapter thirty-one, verse eight.
He goes before you into tomorrow.
He is with you in this quiet now.
He will never leave you... never forsake you.
He will be with you.
Let those words settle over you like a blanket.
He will be with you.
Not might be.
Not could be.
Will be.
His promise does not change with your feelings.
It does not waver when you feel small, or forgotten.
He is here.
Breathe slowly again.
Feel the stillness around you.
In this stillness, God is present.
He knows what this day held.
He knows the ache of the evening.
And he has not left you to carry it alone.
You can rest now.
You can let go of the striving, the worry, the need to be strong.
He is strong for you.
He holds this night.
He holds you.
As you close your eyes, know this.
You are seen.
You are known.
You are loved.
And you are not alone tonight.
May the Lord bless you, and keep you.
May he make his face shine upon you, and give you peace.
