Let this be a moment to rest.
Settle into where you are lying.
Let your body sink down into the surface beneath you.
Release the weight of your shoulders.
Let your jaw unclench.
Soften your eyes.
Take a slow breath in through your nose... and let it go gently.
Again, breathe in slowly... and release.
You have carried much today.
The weight of tasks, and thoughts, and worries.
You do not have to carry them into this night.
There is One who invites you to come just as you are.
Tired.
Weary.
Burdened.
He does not ask you to fix yourself first.
He does not require that you arrive strong, or sorted.
He simply says... come.
Breathe in that invitation now.
And as you breathe out... let your striving go.
Picture yourself standing at the edge of a worn path.
The way is gentle, sloping downward.
You begin to walk.
Each step is easy.
The ground is soft beneath your feet.
You are not rushing.
There is no hurry here.
The path leads you toward a place of rest.
Someone is waiting there for you.
He knows your name.
He knows what you have carried.
And he is not troubled by any of it.
Take another slow breath.
As you come closer, you hear his voice.
It is steady, and kind.
Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.
Matthew, chapter eleven, verse twenty-eight.
This is not a command.
It is an offer.
Jesus does not pull you toward himself.
He waits.
He invites.
And when you come... he gives rest.
Not as a reward for effort... but as a gift.
Let yourself receive it now.
Breathe in rest.
Breathe out the weight you have been holding.
You do not need to solve anything tonight.
You do not need to plan, or prepare.
You are held.
You are safe.
The One who spoke the stars into being is watching over you.
He does not sleep.
He does not grow weary.
And he has made space for you to rest.
Let your breathing slow even more.
Let your thoughts drift, like leaves on water.
You can let go now.
The night is a gift.
Sleep is a mercy.
And you are beloved.
May the Lord give you rest this night.
May his peace cover you like a blanket.
May you wake tomorrow, knowing you are his.
Goodnight.
