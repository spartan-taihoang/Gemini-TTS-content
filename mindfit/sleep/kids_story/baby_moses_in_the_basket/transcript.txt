Here we are again, at the quiet end of the day. Get nice and comfy... because tonight's story is about a tiny baby, a little basket, and a big, wide river.
A long, long time ago, in the land of Egypt, there lived a mother who had a brand new baby boy. She loved him very much. But it was not a safe time for little baby boys, and she wanted to keep him safe more than anything in the world.
So she made a plan. She gathered tall reeds from beside the river, and she wove them together, over and under, over and under, into a little basket. She painted it with sticky tar, so that not one drop of water could get in.
Then she wrapped her baby in a soft blanket, and laid him gently inside. She carried the basket down to the river, and set it among the tall reeds at the water's edge, where the water moved slowly and softly.
The baby's big sister, Miriam, hid nearby to watch. She was very quiet. She didn't take her eyes off the little basket, not even for a moment.
The basket rocked gently on the water, like a cradle. The reeds swayed in the breeze. A dragonfly buzzed past. And inside, the baby slept... safe and warm.
Then Miriam heard voices. The princess of Egypt had come down to the river with her helpers. And she saw something floating among the reeds. What could that be?
She sent one of her helpers to bring it to her. She lifted the lid... and there was the baby! He began to cry, and the princess's heart filled with love for him. I will take care of this little one, she said.
Brave Miriam stepped out from her hiding place. Would you like me to find someone to help you care for the baby? she asked. Yes, please, said the princess. And who do you think Miriam ran to get? The baby's very own mother!
So the baby went home with his mother, to be held and fed and loved, until he was big enough to live with the princess. She named him Moses. And God had a very big plan for Moses, all through his life.
Even when Moses was too small to know it, God was watching over him. Floating on the river, God was there. And God watches over you, too, little one. When you are awake, and when you are fast asleep.
Dear God, thank You for watching over baby Moses, and for watching over me. Thank You for the people who love me and keep me safe. Please hold me gently through the night. Amen.
