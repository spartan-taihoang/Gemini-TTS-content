Hello again, my friend. Is your pillow just right? Good. Tonight I have a story that Jesus told, about a shepherd and one little sheep who wandered away.
Once there was a shepherd who had one hundred sheep. One hundred! Big sheep and little sheep. Fluffy sheep and sleepy sheep. And the shepherd knew every single one of them.
Every day, he led them to green grass to eat, and to cool water to drink. And every evening, when the sun went down, he brought them home and counted them, one by one, as they went through the gate.
Ninety-seven. Ninety-eight. Ninety-nine...
The shepherd stopped. He looked around. Ninety-nine? Where was number one hundred?
One little sheep was missing. She had wandered off to nibble some flowers, and then a little farther, and a little farther still... until she looked up, and she couldn't see the others anymore.
Now, the shepherd still had ninety-nine sheep, safe and sound. But he didn't say, Oh well, that's enough. No. He made sure the others were safe, and then he picked up his lantern and went out into the night to find the one that was lost.
He walked over the hills. He looked behind the rocks. He called her name, softly, into the dark. And far away, in a thorny bush, a small, tired voice answered... baa.
The shepherd found her! He untangled her wool from the thorns, very gently. He didn't scold her. He lifted her up onto his shoulders, and carried her all the way home.
When he got home, he called to his friends and neighbors. Come and celebrate with me!
Rejoice with me; for I have found my sheep which was lost.
Jesus told this story so we would know something very important. God is like that shepherd. And you are like that little sheep. If you ever feel far away, or lost, or alone, he comes looking for you. Every single time.
Dear God, thank You for being my Good Shepherd. Thank You that You always know where I am, and You never stop caring for me. Please carry me safely through the night. Amen.
All one hundred sheep are home now. The gate is closed. The stars are shining. And you are safe, too. Goodnight, my friend.
