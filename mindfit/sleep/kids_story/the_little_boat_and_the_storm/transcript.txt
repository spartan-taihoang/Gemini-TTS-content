Good evening, sleepyhead. Snuggle down under your blanket... because tonight's story happens on a little boat, out on a big lake, on a very windy evening.
Once upon a time, a girl named Mia lived with her grandpa in a small blue house beside a lake. Grandpa had a little wooden boat called Daisy, with a white sail and a bell that went ding when the waves rocked it.
One afternoon, Mia and Grandpa took Daisy out onto the water. The lake was smooth and shiny. Ducks paddled by. Mia trailed her fingers in the cool water, and Grandpa hummed a slow, happy song.
But as the sun went lower, the sky turned gray. The wind began to blow. Whoosh. Little waves became bigger waves. Daisy rocked up... and down... and up again. Ding, ding, went the little bell.
Mia held on tight. Grandpa, she said in a small voice, I'm scared. What do we do?
Grandpa didn't shout, and he didn't hurry. He put one warm hand on Mia's shoulder. I'm right here, he said. First, we take a slow breath together. Then we row home, nice and steady.
So they breathed together. In through the nose, slow and deep... and out through the mouth, like blowing out a candle. In... and out. And Mia's heart, which had been beating fast, began to slow down.
Grandpa lowered the sail and took the oars. Row... and rest. Row... and rest. Mia counted with him. One, two. One, two. And little by little, Daisy turned toward home.
Then something wonderful happened. The wind grew softer. The waves grew smaller. The gray clouds drifted apart, and behind them was a sky full of stars. The lake became smooth and still again, like a mirror.
Look, Mia, said Grandpa. Storms always pass. And when they do, the sky is still there, waiting. Mia smiled, and the little bell gave one last, sleepy ding.
Back at the blue house, Grandpa wrapped Mia in a fluffy towel and made her a mug of warm milk. She curled up in bed, listening to the gentle lap of the water outside her window.
Sometimes, little one, our feelings can be like that windy lake. Big worries. Fast heartbeats. But you can do what Mia did. Take a slow breath... remember who loves you... and wait for the calm. It always comes.
Let's breathe like Mia now. Breathe in... and breathe out. Once more. In... and out.
The wind is quiet now. The water is still. And you are safe and warm in your bed. Goodnight, sleepyhead.
