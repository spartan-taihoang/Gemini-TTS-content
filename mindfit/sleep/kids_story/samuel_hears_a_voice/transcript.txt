Hello, little one. Are you all tucked in? Pull your blanket up nice and snug... because tonight's story is about a boy named Samuel, and a voice he heard in the night.
A long, long time ago, there was a boy named Samuel. Samuel did not live in a house like yours. He lived in a big, quiet tent called the house of the Lord, where people came to pray.
Samuel lived there with an old priest named Eli. Eli was very old, with a long white beard, and his eyes could not see very well anymore. So Samuel helped him. He swept the floors. He fetched the water. And every evening, he helped to keep the golden lamp burning.
One night, the lamp was glowing softly, and everything was quiet. Eli was asleep in his room. And Samuel was lying down on his little bed, his eyes growing heavy... just like yours.
Then, in the stillness, Samuel heard a voice. Samuel. Samuel.
Samuel jumped up and ran to Eli. Here I am! You called me. But Eli rubbed his sleepy eyes and said, I did not call you, Samuel. Go back and lie down.
So Samuel went back to his bed and pulled up his blanket. But soon, he heard the voice again. Samuel. And again he ran to Eli. Here I am! And again Eli said, I did not call you. Go back to sleep.
Then it happened a third time. Samuel. And this time, when Samuel came to him, old Eli sat up slowly. He thought for a long moment. And then he understood.
Samuel, said Eli gently, I think it is God who is calling you. Go and lie down. And if you hear the voice again, say this: Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening.
So Samuel went back to his little bed. He lay very, very still. The lamp glowed. The night was quiet. And then he heard it, softly, one more time. Samuel. Samuel.
And Samuel whispered, Speak; for thy servant heareth.
And God spoke to Samuel that night. Samuel was only a boy, but God knew his name. And from that night on, Samuel listened for God's voice, all the days of his life.
Do you know what, little one? God knows your name too. He knows when you are happy, and when you are sad, and when you are snuggled up in bed like you are right now. And He is always near.
Let's say a little prayer together. Dear God, thank You that You know my name. Help me to listen to You, just like Samuel. Please keep me safe tonight, and everyone I love. Amen.
Now close your eyes. The lamp is glowing. The night is quiet. And you are loved. Goodnight, little one. Sleep tight.
