Hello, my friend. Tonight's story is about a bear, a rainy day, and a big red umbrella.
Bruno was a big brown bear who lived at the edge of the forest. He had a round tummy, a soft nose, and his very favorite thing in the whole world... a big red umbrella.
It was the biggest umbrella in the forest. When Bruno held it over his head, he stayed dry from his ears all the way down to his toes. And he loved to hear the rain go pitter, patter, on the top.
One morning, the clouds rolled in, gray and heavy. Pitter, patter, went the rain. So Bruno opened his big red umbrella and set off down the path to visit the pond.
He hadn't gone far when he saw a little rabbit, shivering under a leaf. Her ears were dripping wet. Bruno stopped. Would you like to come under my umbrella? he asked. Oh, yes please, said the rabbit, and she hopped in close beside him.
A little farther on, they met a mouse, trying to stay dry inside an acorn cup. It wasn't working very well. There's room for one more, said Bruno. And the mouse climbed up onto the rabbit's head, warm and dry.
Then they met a hedgehog. And a squirrel. And a very small, very soggy bird. Each time, Bruno said the same thing. Come in. There's room.
But now the umbrella was very crowded. Everyone was squeezed together, nose to tail. And Bruno's big furry back was sticking out in the rain, getting wetter and wetter. Was he sad?
Not one bit. Because under the umbrella, everyone was laughing and talking and telling stories. Bruno had gone out by himself that morning... and now he had six new friends.
When they reached the pond, the rain stopped. The sun peeked out, and a rainbow stretched across the sky. And the little rabbit said, Bruno, you're all wet! Let's dry you off. So everyone snuggled around the big bear until his fur was warm and fluffy again.
From that day on, whenever it rained, Bruno's friends came to find him. And whenever Bruno felt cold, or tired, or lonely, his friends were there for him, too.
Bruno learned something wonderful that day. When you share what you have, even if it's not very much, it doesn't get smaller. It gets bigger. Because kindness always comes back around.
I wonder, my friend... who could you share your umbrella with tomorrow?
That's the end of our story. Goodnight, my friend.
