Welcome to Seven Nights of Rest. This is night two, Still Waters. Last thing at the front door, we say, the day is Yours. Now we walk through to the kitchen, where the tap has stopped running... and the house is growing quiet.
When sleep won't come, people tell us to count sheep. But in the Bible, you are not the one who counts. You are the sheep. The prophet Jeremiah speaks of flocks passing under the hands of the one who counts them, home for the night. And David knew that Shepherd by heart, in Psalm twenty-three.
The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters. He restoreth my soul.
A sheep lying down by the water doesn't know how many are in the flock. It doesn't know where tomorrow's grass is. It only needs to know whose it is. Keeping count is the Shepherd's work... and He does not lose His place.
So let yourself be counted. Imagine His hand resting lightly on your head as you go by. Not hurrying you. Not inspecting you. Only knowing you... this one is Mine. Feel the weight of that hand, and let your body lie down under it.
Good Shepherd, count me in tonight. And count the ones I worry about, wherever they are lying. Lead my thoughts to quiet water, and restore my soul while I sleep. Amen.
Put out the kitchen light. And say the words we have, as slowly as you like. The day is Yours... I am counted. There is nothing left to add up. Goodnight.
