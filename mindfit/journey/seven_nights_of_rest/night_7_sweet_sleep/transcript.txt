Welcome to Seven Nights of Rest. This is night seven, Sweet Sleep. There is one light left, beside your bed. You can leave it on, if the dark is hard for you. And you can open your eyes whenever you need to.
Think back through the house. The door is locked, and the day is handed over. The kitchen is still. The curtains are drawn. The desk and the shelf and the hall are dark. Someone else is awake in this house, and He loves you. So hear this promise, from Proverbs chapter three, verse twenty-four.
When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid: yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet.
It says lie down, and then it says it again... as though to someone who sat back up. Lie down. You don't have to feel brave to do that. Fear may come into the room. It doesn't get to be the one who keeps watch.
So here are all seven, one for every light. The day is Yours. I am counted. I am covered. Sleep is a gift. Not mine to know. Thank You. I will not be afraid.
Lord, thank You for these seven nights, and for all the nights to come. Stay by this bed. Make my sleep sweet... and when I wake, let me still be with You. Amen.
Once more, very softly... and you don't need to reach the end. The day is Yours... I am counted... I am covered... Sleep is a gift... Not mine to know... Thank You... I will not be afraid. Goodnight.
