Welcome to Seven Nights of Rest. This is night one, Lay the Day Down. These are for bedtime, so please don't listen while you drive. Each night, we'll put out one more light in a quiet house... and learn a few words to fall asleep on.
Tonight we begin at the front door. Picture it, with the day still standing just inside. The bags, the shoes, the unfinished jobs. And you, with your hand on the lock, feeling that you'd better stay up and watch over it all. But there is an old song for people like that, in Psalm one hundred twenty-seven, verse one.
Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it: except the Lord keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain.
The builder may put the tools down. The watchman may go home. It was never only your hands holding this up, and never only your eyes keeping it safe. There is a Keeper for the night shift... and He has already arrived.
So hand the day over. Set down what got done, and say, thank You. Set down what went wrong, and say, forgive me, and mend it. Set down what isn't finished... and leave it by the door. It can stand there until morning.
Lord, I give You the keys. Watch over this house, and everyone I love. I am going off duty now. The day is Yours. Amen.
Now put out the light by the door. Let your shoulders sink... and your hands grow heavy. If you wake, you don't need to get up and check. Just say it softly... the day is Yours. Goodnight.
