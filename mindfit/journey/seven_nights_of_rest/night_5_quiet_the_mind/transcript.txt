Welcome to Seven Nights of Rest. This is night five, Quiet the Mind. If your thoughts turn toward harming yourself, please don't wait for sleep. Call your local emergency number or a crisis line now. Otherwise, come with me to the bookshelf... where the big questions are kept.
A tired mind takes down the heaviest books. How will it all turn out? Why did it happen? How can I fix every one of them? David had a kingdom full of questions like that. And he learned to be humble enough to leave some on the shelf. This is Psalm one hundred thirty-one, verse one.
Lord, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty: neither do I exercise myself in great matters, or in things too high for me.
There is such relief in admitting that. You were not made to understand everything, or to run the world from a pillow. Some things belong on God's shelf. He can reach it... and you don't have to.
Let's make that a breath prayer, with no holding and no counting. As you breathe in... not mine to know. As you breathe out... I am Yours. Breathe in... not mine to know. And out... I am Yours. Keep it going gently, on your own.
Lord, these matters are too high for me, and they are not too high for You. Keep them safe. Quiet my mind, and keep me small and near. Amen.
Put out the light by the shelf. The day is Yours... I am counted... I am covered... Sleep is a gift... Not mine to know. Goodnight.
