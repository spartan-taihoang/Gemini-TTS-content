Welcome to Seven Nights of Rest. This is night six, Thankful for Today. We have nearly put the whole house to bed. Tonight we walk slowly down the hall, toward your room... and we look kindly at how you got here.
We usually think of night as the end. The day is used up, and we count what is left. But when the Bible tells of the very first day, it puts things in a surprising order. It's in Genesis chapter one, verse five.
And God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And the evening and the morning were the first day.
Did you notice which comes first? It's the evening. That is one reason many of God's people have begun their day at sundown. Before you do one useful thing, there is darkness, and rest, and God at work. So this isn't only the end of today. It is the beginning of something given.
Count the small mercies backward, starting from your pillow. Whoever made this bed, or this cloth. Thank You. The last kind voice you heard. Thank You. Something you ate. A step that held. The breath you are taking now. Thank You. Keep walking backward, until you grow too drowsy to count.
Father, thank You for all that carried me to this pillow. I saw so little of it. Begin tomorrow without me, while I sleep. Amen.
