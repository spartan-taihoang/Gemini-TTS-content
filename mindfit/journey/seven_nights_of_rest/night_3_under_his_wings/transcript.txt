Welcome to Seven Nights of Rest. This is night three, Under His Wings. The day is Yours... and I am counted. Tonight we go softly from window to window, and draw the curtains. If you aren't safe where you are, please reach someone who can help before you rest.
The dark outside a window can make a worry look very large. Psalm ninety-one doesn't pretend that the night is empty of trouble. It tells us where we can spend it. Listen to how it begins... and then to the picture it gives us, a little later, in verse four.
He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth shall be thy shield and buckler.
Feathers. Not a wall of iron, but something warm, and close, with a heartbeat under it. A wing doesn't explain the storm to the little ones beneath it. It covers them... and it stays.
Draw your blanket up now, and let it be a picture of that wing. Feel it settle along your arms. Then, in your heart, draw it over someone you love. And someone who is alone tonight. Say each name once... and tuck it in. There is room for them all.
Most High God, cover what I cannot protect. Cover this room, and those names, and the trouble I don't understand. Whatever this night holds, let me spend it close to You. Amen.
Put out the light at the window. These three lines are a whole evening prayer, for this night or any night. The day is Yours... I am counted... I am covered. Goodnight.
