Welcome to Seven Nights of Rest. This is night four, He Gives Sleep. The day is Yours. I am counted. I am covered. Tonight we stop at the desk, where one small lamp is still on... because somebody is still trying.
Sometimes we even work at sleeping. We watch the clock. We do the sums. If I drop off now, I'll get five hours. And the harder we try, the further away it goes. The same psalm that sent the watchman home has a word for tired workers, in Psalm one hundred twenty-seven, verse two.
It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: for so he giveth his beloved sleep.
Notice who does the giving. Sleep is His gift, so it can't be earned. And if it comes slowly, you are no less His beloved. Lying awake is not a failing. If it happens night after night, you can ask a doctor for help... and be beloved there, too.
Jesus once told of a farmer who scattered seed, and went to bed. He was speaking of God's kingdom, but we can borrow the picture. The farmer sleeps, and the seed grows, he doesn't know how. Choose one thing you planted today. Leave it in the dark soil with God... and take your hands away.
Giver of sleep, I stop trying. Grow what I cannot grow. I will take rest as it comes, a little or a lot, from Your hand. Amen.
Turn the clock to the wall, and put out the lamp on the desk. The day is Yours... I am counted... I am covered... Sleep is a gift. Goodnight, beloved.
