Welcome to Five Days of Peace. This is day four, Daily Bread. We have three steps now. Here it is. It's Yours. Help me, and thank You. Today we make the worry smaller... by making it only as wide as one day.
Take a moment to find today. What can you hear in it? What is the light like, where you are? Let your body be here, at this hour, and at no other. Breathe in the air of this day... and let it out.
So much of what exhausts us hasn't happened yet. We attend next week's appointment a dozen times. We have next month's argument in the shower. We try to live all of those days with the strength that was given for this one. No wonder we are tired. One person was never meant to live a whole year at once.
Jesus once told a worried crowd to look up at the birds. They don't plant, He said, or harvest, or fill any barns... and your heavenly Father feeds them. A bird is busy all day long. It simply isn't busy with Thursday. Then He said this, in Matthew chapter six, verse thirty-four.
Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.
In that older English, taking thought meant worrying, being pulled apart by care. He isn't telling you never to plan. He is honest that each day has trouble of its own. He just won't let you carry two days' trouble on one day's bread.
So let's give today some edges. With one finger, on your leg or in the palm of your hand, draw a small square. Four sides. Slowly. That square is today. It starts where you woke, and it ends where you next lie down to rest. Nothing else has to fit inside it.
Now, what truly belongs in the square? Look at your worry, and find the piece of it that can be done today. One call. One form. One meal, one kindness, one honest rest. Set that piece inside. If there is no piece at all for today... then the square is allowed to be empty.
And the rest of it, set outside the line. The answer you don't have. The day you are dreading. All the what ifs. You aren't throwing them away. You are leaving them in tomorrow, which is where they live... and where God already is.
I can't promise you that tomorrow will be easy, and neither does this verse. It promises a Father who knows what you need. When that day becomes a today, it will have its own square, and its own bread. You will not arrive there before Him.
Here is our fourth step, joined to the others. Palms up... here it is. Shoulders down... it's Yours. Hand on your heart... help me, and thank You. And then the little square, drawn with one finger... just today.
Let's pray. Our Father, give me this day my daily bread, and the grace to eat it without staring at tomorrow's plate. Show me the piece that is mine to do. Watch over everything outside the line. And meet me in the next day, when it comes. Amen.
As you go, when your thoughts run on ahead, you don't have to chase them. Draw the square. On a table, on the steering wheel when you've parked, in the pocket of your coat. And tell your busy heart, very kindly... just today.
