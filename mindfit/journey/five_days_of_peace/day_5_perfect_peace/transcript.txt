Welcome to Five Days of Peace. This is day five, Perfect Peace. It's the last day of this journey, and I'm glad you are here. We have one step left... and it's the one where you get to stop holding yourself up.
Find something you can lean on. The back of a chair. A wall. A pillow, or the arm of a sofa. Don't give it your weight just yet. Only know that it's there, behind you. Let your breath come and go.
Worry keeps us very upright. Even lying down, some part of us is sitting forward, braced, watching the door. We are holding up the people we love, and the future, and ourselves. And we call it being responsible. But a body can't rest in that position... and neither can a soul.
Isaiah wrote down a song for God's people to sing. It's about a strong city, with salvation for its walls. And in the middle of that song, among all that stone, there is a line about the human mind. It is Isaiah chapter twenty-six, verse three.
Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.
That word, stayed, means propped. Held up by leaning on something stronger. And where our Bible says perfect peace, the Hebrew simply says the word twice. Shalom, shalom. Peace, peace. As if once were not enough for someone as tired as you.
So now, let the wall have you. Slowly, give your back to what is behind you. A little weight. A little more. Feel the exact moment when you are no longer the one doing the holding. Let your mind do the same thing, toward God. It doesn't have to be empty. It only has to lean.
You will sit forward again. Everybody does. A stayed mind is not a mind that never moves. It's a mind that knows where the wall is, and keeps coming back to rest on it. Each return is trust... even the hundredth one.
And you know the way back now. We spread the letter out where He could read it. We took off the heavy coat. We turned our worry into asking, and thanks. We drew the edges of one day. And we leaned. Let's walk through all five, slowly, and then once more as you would in a hard moment.
Turn your palms up. Here it is. Let your shoulders fall. It's Yours. Rest a hand on your heart. Help me, and thank You. Draw the small square. Just today. And lean back, all the way. I lean on You.
Now a little more simply, the way you might in a waiting room, with no one noticing. Palms... here it is. Shoulders... it's Yours. Heart... help me, and thank You. Square... just today. And lean... I lean on You.
Let's pray. Lord, my strong city, I lay my weight on You. Keep in perfect peace this mind that wanders and comes home. When worry returns, remind my hands what to do. And thank You that beneath every step I have learned, it was always You holding me. Amen.
The journey ends here, and the way back stays open. Five steps, less than a minute, as many times a day as you need. Thank you for walking these days with me. May the peace of God keep you... peace, peace.
