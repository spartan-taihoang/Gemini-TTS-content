Welcome to Five Days of Gratitude. This is day five, Enough for Today. It's the last day of this journey, and I'm so glad you're here. We have one sentence left to learn... and then we'll put all five together.
Let yourself slow down. Let both hands rest open, if that's comfortable. Breathe in only as much air as you need. Notice that you don't store it up. You let it go... and there is more.
All around us, there's a quiet voice that says, not yet. Not enough saved. Not enough done. Not young enough, or clever enough, or far enough along. It moves the line each time we get near it. And a heart that is always chasing the line finds it very hard to say thank you.
When Jesus taught His friends to pray, He gave them words for this. He could have taught them to ask for a storehouse. He taught them to ask for something that fits in one hand. It is in Matthew chapter six, verse eleven.
Give us this day our daily bread.
This day. Not the whole year, all at once. Daily bread. Not a feast, and not crumbs. Bread. And listen... He says us. Give us. Our bread. So this kind of enough is never just mine. It has room at the table for someone else.
If you truly don't have enough today, this verse does not scold you. It is a prayer for people who need bread. You can ask Him plainly for what is missing, and you can let others help to bring it. Being content doesn't mean you stop asking. It means you can ask with an open hand.
So ask, just for today. What is the bread you need before this day ends? Food, or money for it. Patience. An answer. A little courage. Ask Him for that one portion, in your own words. And then notice what is already on the table. What has He given that is enough for this very minute?
Think of the road these days have taken. We came in through the gate, and began with who God is. We followed a plain thing home to Him. We opened one hand in the middle of what was hard. We gave our thanks to a person, out loud. Whether you walked every step of that or only some, you can pray where it has led.
Here is the fifth sentence. It is enough. Now all five, slowly. Let each one have its own breath. You are good. This is from You. Even here. Through them. It is enough.
Once more, and this time they are yours. I'll say each one softly, and you can pray it after me. You are good... This is from You... Even here... Through them... It is enough.
Let's pray. Our Father, give us this day our daily bread. Thank You for who You are, for what You send, for staying in the hard places, and for the people You love me through. When I forget, bring me back to the gate. And make my enough big enough to share. Amen.
The journey ends here, but the prayer can come with you. Choose one meal a day, and say your five sentences before the first bite. It will take less time than the food takes to cool. Thank you for walking these days with me. Go in peace... and go with thanks.
