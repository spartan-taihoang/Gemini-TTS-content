Welcome to Walking Through Grief. This is day three, Comfort That Lasts. Walk with me... remember with me. Today there is nothing to carry, and nothing to give. Only something to receive, if you can.
Paul knew what it was to be crushed. He once told his friends that the weight had been far beyond his strength, until he despaired of life itself. He doesn't hurry past that. And in the same letter, he gives God this name, in Second Corinthians chapter one, verses three and four.
Blessed be God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies, and the God of all comfort; Who comforteth us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble, by the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God.
One day, you may sit beside someone else who is grieving, and know not to say very much. But that is not your task today. Your loss didn't happen to make you useful. The comfort comes first... and it is for you.
So let a little of it in. Think of one kindness that has reached you. A meal at the door. A hand on your back. A message that needed no answer. If you can't find one, rest your own hand over your heart... and let God begin there.
God of all comfort, I am tired of being strong. Come as softly as You need to. Send me people who can stay, and help me to let them. Comfort me. Amen.
The next time someone offers you something small, you could try saying yes. A cup of tea. A ride. Ten minutes of company. And under your breath, the prayer... comfort me. Go gently.
