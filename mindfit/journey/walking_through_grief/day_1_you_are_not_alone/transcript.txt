Welcome to Walking Through Grief. This is day one, You Are Not Alone. I'm so sorry for your loss. We'll walk slowly, and you can stop whenever you need to. If grief ever makes you think of harming yourself, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line, or reach someone who can be with you.
After Jesus died, two of His friends set out on a road to a village called Emmaus. They walked, and they talked, and they were sad. And Jesus Himself came near, and walked along with them. They didn't recognize Him. But He was no less there. Long before that road, Psalm thirty-four, verse eighteen had said where He could be found.
The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.
So you may not recognize Him today. Grief can do that to our eyes. It doesn't mean you've been left to walk this by yourself. You don't have to catch up with God... He has fallen into step with you.
On that road, He asked them why they were sad. And then He let them talk. So tell Him, as you would tell someone walking beside you. Their name. What happened. Or only, I don't have words yet. He isn't in any hurry.
Lord Jesus, I can't always see You on this road. Match Your step to mine. Stay when I'm quiet, and stay when I fall apart. Walk with me. Amen.
When you next have to get up and cross a room, let those be your words. Just three, at the speed of your feet... walk with me. Go gently.
