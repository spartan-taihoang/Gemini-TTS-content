Welcome to Walking Through Grief. This is day four, A Future and a Hope. It's the last day of this journey, though not the end of your grief. Nobody is asking you to be finished. We'll only lift our eyes a little way along the road.
The prophet Jeremiah once wrote to people who had lost their home, and the life they knew. He didn't tell them it would soon be over. It would be seventy years. He told them to build houses there, to plant gardens, and to eat. And in that same letter, chapter twenty-nine, verse eleven, he gave them these words from God.
For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the Lord, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end.
It was a promise made to grieving people, far from what they loved. It doesn't explain your loss, or call it good. It says that God's thoughts toward you are still peace. You can miss them all your life... and still be led into a life.
A garden begins very small. Is there one seed you could plant, without leaving them behind? A walk. A recipe of theirs. A day marked kindly on the calendar. If it's too soon, leave the seed in His hand. It will keep.
Lord, I can't see very far. Take my love, and my sorrow, and the days I don't know how to live. Give me enough light for the next step. Lead me on. Amen.
Here are your four prayers for the road. Walk with me. Remember with me. Comfort me. Lead me on. Come back to any of these days, in any order, whenever you need them. You do not walk alone.
