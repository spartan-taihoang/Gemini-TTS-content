Welcome to Walking Through Grief. This is day two, Tears Are Heard. We began with three words for the road... walk with me. Today is for everything you carry along it, that other people may have stopped asking about.
In the first days, people often ask to hear the story. Then the world moves on, and you're left holding all of it. The last conversation. The way they laughed. The small things only you would know. It can feel as if you're the only one keeping the record. David found that he wasn't, in Psalm fifty-six, verse eight.
Thou tellest my wanderings: put thou my tears into thy bottle: are they not in thy book?
God has a book. What you cried is written in it, and what you couldn't cry. So you can put the pen down for a while. If you forget a detail, it isn't lost. If you have a better hour, you haven't been unfaithful. He remembers with you.
Choose one thing about them that you don't want forgotten. It can be very small. How they said your name. What they always ordered. The shape of their hands. Tell it to God slowly, as if He were writing it down.
Father, hear my tears as prayers. Keep what I am afraid of losing. Hold their story, and hold mine, and let me rest from keeping it all. Remember with me. Amen.
You can always return to these two prayers, however far you go on this road. When you can't see Him... walk with me. When you're afraid of forgetting... remember with me. Your tears are heard. Go softly.
