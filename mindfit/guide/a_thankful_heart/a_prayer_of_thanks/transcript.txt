Welcome to A Thankful Heart. This is A Prayer of Thanks. For the next few minutes, we will simply pray our way through a day, from morning to night. I'll lend you some words. You can keep the ones that fit.
Come to rest for a moment, wherever this finds you. It doesn't matter what time it is. A whole day can fit inside a prayer. Let your hands be open, if you like... and let your breathing become unhurried.
There is a song in the Bible that was written for the Sabbath day, when work was set down, and there was time to look back at what God had done. It is Psalm ninety-two. This is how it opens, in verses one and two.
It is a good thing to give thanks unto the Lord, and to sing praises unto thy name, O most High: To shew forth thy lovingkindness in the morning, and thy faithfulness every night.
Love in the morning. Faithfulness every night. As if thanks were a thread, tied at both ends of the day, so that nothing in between falls outside of His care. Let's follow that thread now, and pray.
Lord of the morning, thank You for waking me, on the days I sprang up, and the days I could hardly rise. Thank You for the first light, for water on my face, for the first thing I tasted. Before I had done one useful thing, I was already loved. For Your lovingkindness in the morning... I give You thanks.
Lord of the working hours, thank You for what my hands are able to do, and for rest when they cannot. Thank You for work that mattered, and dull work that fed someone. For the mistakes that were forgiven, and the help that came when I didn't know how to ask. For strength enough for the middle of the day... I give You thanks.
Lord of every table, thank You for the people You have set around my life. The ones who are easy to love, and the ones who are teaching me how. The ones I miss. The ones whose names I never learned, who grew my food, and cleaned the floors, and kept watch while I slept. For every kindness that reached me through another person... I give You thanks.
Pause here, and add one name of your own. Someone this prayer would not be complete without. Say the name silently... and let it rest for a breath in the hands of God. For them, Lord... I give You thanks.
Lord of the evening, thank You for the door that closes behind me, and the work that can wait. Thank You for what went well. And for what did not, thank You that it does not have the last word. You were there in the parts I would live again, and in the parts I would not. For Your faithfulness every night... I give You thanks.
And Lord of my whole life, thank You for what is older than this day. For every time You carried me when I only thought I was walking. For mercy I did not earn, and for more grace than I could ever count. For Jesus, and for the love that will not let me go... I give You thanks. Amen.
There may be days when you mean every word of that prayer, and days when you can mean only one line. Both are prayer. It is still a good thing to give thanks... however small the voice you bring to it.
Tie the thread at both ends, if you can. One thank You when you first open your eyes. And one before you close them again. The day in between belongs to Him. Go with a thankful heart.
