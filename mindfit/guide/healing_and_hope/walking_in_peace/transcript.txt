Welcome to Healing and Hope. This practice is called Walking in Peace. You can listen sitting still, or lying down, or on a slow walk. Peace can travel at any speed.
Wherever you are, find the places where you meet the world. The soles of your feet, perhaps. Your back, or your hands. Let your breath fall into an easy rhythm... like footsteps that have nowhere they must be by a certain hour.
We sometimes think peace will come when the healing is finished. When the grief is over, and the scar has faded, and we are who we were before. But some losses go on being losses. Some bodies mend slowly, or only in part. Peace doesn't wait for all of that to be settled. It is something you can be given... on the way.
An old priest named Zechariah learned this. He had waited most of a lifetime for a child, and then spent long months unable to speak a word. When his son was born, and his voice came back, he sang about the mercy of God... coming like sunrise. His song ends this way, in Luke chapter one, verse seventy-nine.
To give light to them that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace.
He gives light to those who are sitting. They haven't found their way out. They haven't even stood up yet. The morning comes to them where they are. And then, so tenderly, He takes care of their feet... and guides them into the way of peace.
So let's walk a little of that way, in your heart or with your feet. One slow step, for where you have been. Lord, You were there. One slow step, for where you are. Lord, You are here. One slow step, for what you cannot see yet. Lord, You will be there.
As you walk, what would you like to carry more lightly? You don't have to put down the love, or the memory. Those can come with you. But perhaps there is some blame. A question of what you should have done. A date by which you thought you had to be well. If you are willing, let one of those rest at the side of the road.
There will be mornings when you limp. That is still walking. There will be days you need to sit in the dark again for a while. He knows how to find you there. You can laugh without betraying what you lost. You can cry without losing the ground you have gained. Both belong on the way of peace.
Let's pray. God of tender mercy, thank You for coming to me where I sit. Shine on the next few feet of the road. Heal what can be healed, and hold what cannot yet be. Give me companions for the way... and guide my feet, my slow and faithful feet, into Your peace. Amen.
May the Lord give you light for the step in front of you. May He give you mercy for the steps behind. And wherever the road goes from here... may you walk it in His peace.
