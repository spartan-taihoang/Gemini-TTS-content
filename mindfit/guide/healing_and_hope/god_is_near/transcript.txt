Welcome to Healing and Hope. This practice is called God Is Near. Whatever has broken, and however long ago it broke, you can bring it here. You don't have to explain it first.
Let yourself be held up by the chair, or the floor, or whatever is beneath you. If some part of you is hurting, there's no need to force it to relax. Just give it a little room. Breathe in as far as is comfortable... and let the breath go.
When we are in pain, it can seem that God must be somewhere else. With the people who are singing, perhaps. The people whose prayers were answered. But when you can't feel Him, it doesn't mean that He has left you. Pain can fill a room so completely... that it's hard to see who is sitting beside us.
Long ago, God spoke to a people who had lost a great deal, and were afraid of what was still to come. He didn't tell them there would be no deep water. He told them who would be in it with them. It is written in Isaiah forty-three, verse two.
When thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow thee.
When you pass through. Not around. Not high above, on some bridge for stronger people. Through. And there, with the water cold against you, and the far bank out of sight... I will be with thee.
What are the waters for you, just now? A diagnosis? An ending you didn't choose? A slow recovery that others have stopped asking about? You don't need to wade any deeper to answer. From where you stand, simply name it to Him... this is my water, Lord.
Now rest one hand in the other, if you are able. Feel the warmth where they meet. Let it remind you of a hand you cannot see. As you breathe in, hear Him say... I will be with thee. As you breathe out, answer with as little as you have... here I am. And again... I will be with thee. Here I am.
A child being carried across a stream doesn't have to know how deep it is. She doesn't have to find the stones with her feet. She may even cry the whole way over. It changes nothing about the arms that are carrying her.
So if your grip is weak today, that is all right. Your safety was never in how tightly you could hold on. He is near when you feel it, and near when you don't. And sometimes His nearness has a human face... a nurse, a friend, someone who sits down and stays. You are allowed to let them.
Let's pray. Lord, You see the water I am standing in. I don't ask to understand it all. I ask You to be close. When I cannot feel You, keep hold of me still. Bring me kind hands, and give me the humility to take them. Amen.
Carry four words with you from here. I will be... with thee. Say them at the edge of whatever comes next. Go gently. You do not cross alone.
