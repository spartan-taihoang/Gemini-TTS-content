Welcome to Healing and Hope. This practice is called Small Steps Forward. You won't be asked to feel better by the end of it. We are only looking for the next small thing.
Wherever you are, stop for a moment. Let your shoulders come down by the width of a finger. Let your jaw loosen. Breathe out slowly... and notice that for this one breath, you are not required to go anywhere.
There was a prophet named Elijah, who had been very brave for a very long time. Then one day, his courage ran out. He walked into the wilderness, sat down beneath a juniper tree, and told God that he had had enough. And then, worn all the way through, he fell asleep.
He was not given a speech. He was not scolded. An angel touched him, and there by his head was bread, baked on the coals, and a jar of water. He ate, and he drank, and he lay down again. And God let him. Then, in First Kings chapter nineteen, verse seven, we read this.
And the angel of the Lord came again the second time, and touched him, and said, Arise and eat; because the journey is too great for thee.
The journey is too great for thee. What a kind thing to hear. Not, you should be further along by now. Not, others would manage this. It is too much... so here is bread. Heaven's plan for an exhausted man began with a meal, and sleep, and a gentle hand.
Healing often begins that way. It is rarely a straight road. There are days you walk, and days you lie down again under the tree. A harder day doesn't erase the steps you have taken. And rest is not the opposite of going forward. Sometimes, it is the step.
So what is your bread and water today? Something small enough to be done while you are still tired. A glass of water. A shower. Opening the curtain. Taking the medicine, or keeping the appointment. Answering one message with, I'm having a hard day. Let one of them come to mind... only one.
Now picture yourself doing it, slowly. Where your hands will be. What you will touch first. If it still feels too great, make it smaller. Not the shower... just the towel. Not the walk... just the shoes. When it is small enough, give it to God, and say... with You, this much.
This much is enough for today. You don't have to see all forty days of the road. Elijah couldn't, beneath his tree. And you don't have to walk it unhelped. A doctor, a counselor, a friend with a pot of soup... any of them may be how the bread arrives.
Let's pray. Father, You know when the journey is too great for me. Feed me with what I need for this day. Let me rest without shame, and rise without hurry. Bless the one small step I can take... and meet me again for the next. Amen.
When you're ready, take your one small thing with you. Do it gently, and let it count. And when you have done it... you may rest.
