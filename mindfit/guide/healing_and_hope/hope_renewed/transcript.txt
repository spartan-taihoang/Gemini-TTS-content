Welcome to Healing and Hope. This practice is called Hope Renewed. If hope feels far too large a word today, don't worry. We will begin with a very small piece of it.
Settle in, as gently as you can. Soften the muscles around your eyes. Breathe in, without pulling at the air... and breathe out, the way you might near a candle you didn't want to disturb.
After a loss, or a long illness, hope can start to feel dangerous. You hoped before, and it hurt. So you learn to expect less, and to call that being sensible. There is no blame for that here. A heart that has been bruised will be careful where it's touched.
Isaiah knew about bruised things. He wrote of a servant whom God would send, someone who would not shout, or push, or break down doors. And the Gospel of Matthew tells us that this servant is Jesus. Here is how gentle He is, in Isaiah forty-two, verse three.
A bruised reed shall he not break, and the smoking flax shall he not quench.
A reed was a common, hollow thing. If one was bent, you snapped it, and picked another. And flax was the wick of a little oil lamp. When it burned down to smoke, it was no use for seeing by. The sensible thing was to pinch it out.
But He doesn't snap the reed. He doesn't pinch out the wick. He bends close to what is nearly finished... cups His hands around it... and waits with it. A bruised reed is safe with Him. So are you.
Is there any smoke at all, in you? It needn't be a flame. A song you still half like. A person whose voice you don't mind hearing. A faint wish to see the sea again, or a garden in spring. Even the wish that you could hope... is a thread of smoke. See if you can find one.
Don't blow on it. Don't ask it to become a fire. Just let His hands be around it, and breathe easily beside it. Breathing in... You will not break me. Breathing out... You will not put me out. Once more. You will not break me. You will not put me out.
Christian hope is not a promise that everything will go the way we ask. It is the promise that Jesus has walked into death itself, and out the other side... and that nothing you have lost, and no part of you that is broken, is beyond His reach. You don't have to hold that hope perfectly. It is strong enough to hold you.
Let's pray. Gentle Jesus, here is what is bent in me, and here is what is barely burning. Be careful with it, as only You can. Guard my small hope from the wind. And in Your time, and at Your pace, let there be light in me again. Amen.
As you go, watch for one small light. A window, a lamp, the screen of a phone with a kind word on it. Let it say to you, quietly... not quenched. Go in hope, however small.
