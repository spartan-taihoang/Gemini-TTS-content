Welcome to Grief and Comfort. This practice is called One Small Step. It isn't a step away from the one you lost. You will never be asked for that here. It is only a step that you and your love can take together.
Feel the ground beneath you, or whatever is holding you up. Press down very lightly, with a heel or a hand... and let it go. That is how small we mean. Nothing today needs to be any bigger than that.
Grief can make us wander. We walk into a room and forget why. We drive the old way without thinking. We pick things up and put them down. It can feel like going in circles, and getting nowhere at all. And it's easy to believe that no one is keeping track of any of it.
David knew what it was to wander. He was running for his life, far from home, frightened and alone among strangers. And in that place he said something astonishing about God. It is written in Psalm fifty-six, verse eight.
Thou tellest my wanderings: put thou my tears into thy bottle: are they not in thy book?
To tell, in that old English, can mean to count. God counts the wanderings. Every circle around the kitchen. Every walk that went nowhere. And the tears... He gathers them, the way a traveler saves water in dry country. Not one step of this has been wasted on Him. Not one tear has fallen unrecorded.
So you don't have to take a step in order to be seen. You are seen already. But love often longs for somewhere to go, when the person it belonged to is gone. And sometimes it helps to give it one small, kind thing to do.
I'll offer a few, and you can leave every one of them, if it's too soon. You might light a candle, and say their name. You might write them one line, beginning, I wanted to tell you. You might tell someone a story about them. Cook the thing they liked. Or do one kindness that they would have done. Is there one that your heart doesn't turn away from?
If one has come to you, hold it loosely. Picture the first moment of it... the match, the pen, the name in your mouth. If it makes you cry, that doesn't mean it was the wrong step. Now offer it to God, just as small as it is. Lord, here is my love. Show it where to go.
And if today's step is only to get through today, He counts that one too. But if getting through starts to feel impossible, or you think of harming yourself, please reach for someone now. Your local emergency number. A crisis line. A person who can come and be with you. Asking for help is a step... and it is a brave one.
Let's pray. God who counts my wanderings, thank You for keeping what I have cried, and where I have been. I don't know how to live all the days that are ahead. So give me this one. Bless the small step I take in love. Walk slowly with me, for I am slow. And keep us all in Your care, the living and those we miss, until every tear is wiped away. Amen.
Go as slowly as you like. You can carry them with you, and still take a step. And wherever your feet wander next... it will be counted, and it will be kept, by the God who loves you.
