Welcome to Calming an Anxious Heart. This practice is called Breathe and Be Still. If your heart is racing, or your body feels ready to run, you are in the right place. We'll slow down a little at a time.
Start with what is solid. Press your feet softly into the floor, or your hands into whatever is beneath them. Feel it push back. It is holding. Press once more... and let go.
An anxious body is trying to protect you. It quickens the breath and tightens the muscles, as if there were something to fight, or somewhere to flee. You don't need to be angry with it. You can thank it for trying... and show it that this moment can be slower.
God's people once stood in a place with nowhere to run. The sea was in front of them, and an army was coming up behind. They were very afraid. And Moses said to them, in Exodus, chapter fourteen, verse fourteen...
The Lord shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace.
Hold your peace. They still had a sea to walk through, and soon they would take the next step. But the saving did not depend on their strength. Before they moved at all, God had already taken their side.
Let's give those words to your breath. We won't take big breaths, only soft ones, with a longer breath out. If that ever feels uncomfortable, breathe as you normally do, and simply listen. There is no wrong way to rest here.
Breathe in softly... the Lord will fight for me. And breathe out slowly... I can hold my peace. Again, breathe in... the Lord will fight for me. And out, all the way... I can hold my peace.
Keep the breath easy, and let the words grow shorter. Breathe in... He fights. Breathe out... I am still. Breathe in... He fights. Breathe out, long and slow... I am still.
Now, as you keep breathing, let one part of you stand down. Your eyebrows, which have been on watch. Your tongue, resting in your mouth. The muscles of your stomach. The space between your shoulders. They don't all have to soften. One is a beginning.
You may still feel some of the fear. That doesn't mean this isn't working, and it doesn't mean your faith is small. The people by the sea were trembling when those words were spoken to them. God did not wait for them to feel brave before He helped them.
Take one more breath with the whole verse. The Lord shall fight for you... and ye shall hold your peace. For this minute, there is no argument you have to win... and no future you have to defend.
Let's pray. Lord, my defender, my body is tired of bracing. Stand where I have been standing guard. Slow what is racing in me. When it is time to take a step, show me the step. Until then, teach me to be still beside You. Amen.
You can take this with you into the next hard moment, in a hallway, in the car, or in a waiting room. Feet on the ground. A long breath out. And three quiet words... hold your peace. Go gently.
