Welcome to Calming an Anxious Heart. This practice is called Cast Your Cares. You may have been carrying something for days, or for years. Let's take a few minutes to share the weight of it.
Settle into a position that asks very little of you. Let your arms be heavy. Turn your hands so the palms can rest. Breathe in, just as much as you need... and breathe out, as if you were lowering something carefully to the ground.
Have you ever carried heavy bags a long way, and refused every offer of help? After a while, your fingers curl around the handles. Even when you reach the door, it takes a moment to open them. We can carry worry like that for so long... that holding on begins to feel like the safe thing.
David carried a worry that had a face. In Psalm fifty-five, he has been hurt by a close friend, and he says he wishes he had wings like a dove, so he could fly away. He can't fly away. But he finds something else he can do. He tells us in verse twenty-two.
Cast thy burden upon the Lord, and he shall sustain thee: he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.
He shall sustain thee. Notice that God takes hold of more than the burden. He takes hold of you. Even if the trouble is still here tomorrow, you will not be standing under it alone.
So what have you been holding? Perhaps a person you can't change. A decision you can't see the end of. Money, or health, or a child you love. Choose one burden for now, just one, and let it come to mind.
Picture it as something in your hands. It might be a stone, or a box, or a bag with a worn strap. Feel how much it weighs. You can be honest about that. Lord, this is heavy. I have been holding it for a long time.
Now imagine His hands beneath yours. You don't have to throw it, or be sure, or be strong. Just lower it a little, until He has some of the weight. Breathe out... and lower it a little more. Breathe out... and loosen one finger. Then another.
And when you are ready, let your hands open. Lord, I give You the outcome. I give You what other people choose. I give You tomorrow. I keep only the next small, loving thing that is mine to do.
You may pick it up again in an hour. Most of us do. That isn't failure. It only means you can hand it back again, as many times as you need to. He will not grow tired of receiving it. And sometimes He sustains us through other hands... a friend, a pastor, a doctor, a counselor. You can let them help as well.
Listen again, with empty hands. Cast thy burden upon the Lord... and he shall sustain thee. Sustain. To hold up. To keep going. That is what He is doing for you, even now.
Let's pray. Faithful God, I hand You the burden I have pictured here. Care for it better than I can. Hold me up where I am worn thin, and keep my heart from being swept away. When I reach for the weight again, gently remind me whose it is. Amen.
Before you go, open and close your hands once, slowly. Let them remember how it feels to be open. They are free now, for the next small thing. Go lightly.
