Welcome to Finding Rest. This practice is called Resting in Silence. I'll say a little, and then less. Between my words there will be some quiet, and you don't have to fill it.
Settle where you are. Let the surface beneath you take your weight. Notice the sounds that are already here... something humming, something passing outside, your own soft breath. None of them needs anything from you. They can stay.
Silence can feel strange at first. When the noise stops, our thoughts sometimes get louder. But think of two old friends on a porch at dusk. They can sit for a long while without a word... and neither one wonders if they are still loved.
Rest with God can be like that. David knew it, even while others were working against him. In Psalm sixty-two, verse five, he turns to his own soul and says these words.
My soul, wait thou only upon God; for my expectation is from him.
Wait thou only upon God. Waiting is not performing. You don't have to feel something special, or hear an answer. You only have to be here, with the One who is already here.
Choose one small word to return to. It might be Father... or here... or wait. When a thought pulls you away, don't push against it. Let it pass, and gently say your word again.
We'll begin with just a few breaths of quiet. Let your face be soft, and your hands be still. I'll speak again in a moment. Until then... simply wait with Him.
That's enough. However it went, it was enough. If your mind was crowded, God was with you in the crowd. If it was calm, He was with you in the calm.
This time, the quiet will be a little longer. There is nothing to achieve in it. If your word comes to mind, say it once. If it doesn't, you can rest without it. You are allowed... to simply be held.
Still here. And He is still here. My soul, wait thou only upon God. You haven't had to explain yourself, or prove a thing... and you have not been alone for one moment of it.
God of all quiet, thank You that I can be with You without words. Receive my silence as a prayer. Receive my tiredness as trust. And when I can no longer keep my attention on You, keep Yours on me. Amen.
Now I'll leave you with one last space of quiet. You don't need to wait for me after this. If sleep comes while you are resting, let it come. It is safe to drift away in His company.
Wait thou only upon God. There is nothing more you need to say tonight. Rest now.
