Welcome to Finding Rest. This practice is called Breathing Slowly. It is a simple thing, and you already know how to do it. We'll just do it more gently, and with God in mind.
Find a position that is easy to stay in. Let your eyes close, or rest them on one quiet spot. Before you change anything at all, notice the breath you already have. Where do you feel it? In your nose... your chest... or low in your belly?
Most of the day, we never think about breathing. It carries on through meetings and traffic, through worry and laughter. All day long, you have been given breath after breath... and you didn't have to earn a single one.
The Bible begins our story with that gift. Before the first person had done any work, or said any prayer, God drew near. We read it in Genesis, chapter two, verse seven.
And the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.
The breath of life. It came from Him first. So this breath is more than air. It is a small reminder that you are made, and known, and kept.
We'll breathe in for a slow count of three, and out for a count of four. There is no need to fill your lungs all the way. If the count doesn't suit you, let it go and follow your own rhythm. Comfort matters more than counting.
Breathe in... one... two... three. And out... one... two... three... four. Again, breathe in... one... two... three. And out... one... two... three... four.
Now give the breath a few words. As you breathe in... You give me life. As you breathe out... I give You my day. In again... You give me life. And out... I give You my day.
And let the words grow smaller. Breathe in... given. Breathe out... released. Breathe in... given. Breathe out... released.
Your mind may have wandered off while we were counting. That is all right. It's what minds do, especially tired ones. You haven't failed at this. The next breath is simply another place to begin, and there is always kindness waiting in it.
Now stop steering, and let your breathing find its own pace. Feel your chest rise, without being told. Feel it fall, without any help from you. Even when you are asleep, this will go on. You can stop being in charge for a while. The One who gave the breath will watch over it.
Let's pray. Creator God, thank You for the breath You have given me today. Where I have been hurried, slow me. Where I have been tight and afraid, soften me. Let every breath tonight rest in Your care, even the ones I will never notice. Amen.
If you wake in the night, you can come straight back to this. Nothing to work out, and nothing to solve. Only a breath in... given. And a breath out... released. Sleep gently.
