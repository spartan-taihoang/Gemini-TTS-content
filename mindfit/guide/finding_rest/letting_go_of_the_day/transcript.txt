Welcome to Finding Rest. This practice is called Letting Go of the Day. There is nothing you need to finish here. For the next few minutes, you only need to set some things down.
Let yourself settle, sitting or lying down. Feel what is holding you up... the bed, the chair, the floor beneath it all. You don't have to hold yourself quite so tightly. Take an easy breath in... and let it go.
The body often stops before the day does. You switch off the light, and there it is again... the message you didn't answer, the thing you said at lunch, the job that is only half done. It's as if the day followed you down the hall, still talking.
Long ago, someone who had been through great trouble learned to speak kindly to their own soul. They had cried out to God, and they had been heard. These are their words, from Psalm one hundred sixteen, verse seven.
Return unto thy rest, O my soul; for the Lord hath dealt bountifully with thee.
Return unto thy rest. Return... as if rest is a home you already have. You don't have to build it tonight. You only have to turn back toward it.
And notice the reason. It isn't that everything got done. It is that the Lord has been good. Your rest can lean on His goodness, even when your list is unfinished.
So let's walk gently back through your day, and leave it with Him. Begin with the morning. See the first room you stood in, and the first thing that needed you. Don't judge it. Just notice it... and as you breathe out, say quietly within, Lord, the morning is Yours.
Now the middle of the day. The faces you saw. The work in your hands. Perhaps a moment that went well, and a moment you would like to have back. Let both of them be there. Breathe in softly... and as you breathe out, Lord, the afternoon is Yours.
And now this evening. Whatever was said, and whatever was left unsaid. The dishes, the door, the last thing you looked at before you came here. Breathe in once more... and as you breathe out, Lord, the evening is Yours.
Is there one thing that still doesn't want to be put down? You can give it a name. Then picture yourself laying it on a table beside you. It will be safe there. If it needs you tomorrow, you can pick it up with rested hands.
Now let the body follow. Let your forehead smooth. Let your jaw loosen. Let your shoulders drop away from your ears. Let your hands open... just a little. Open hands have finished carrying.
Let's pray. Father, thank You for every kindness in this day, the ones I saw and the ones I missed. Forgive what I did poorly. Keep what I could not finish. I place this whole day in Your care, and I return to my rest in You. Amen.
If the day starts talking again, you don't need to argue with it. Just breathe out, and answer softly... return unto thy rest, O my soul. The day is in good hands now. Rest well.
