Let your eyes close gently now.
Let the day fall away behind you.
Feel the weight of your body against the bed.
Your shoulders, your back, your legs... each part held, and safe.
Draw in a slow breath.
Let it go, without hurry.
Again, breathing in... and out.
Tonight, we walk with the shepherd through the oldest night prayer.
The twenty-third psalm.
A song sung by David under stars... sung by travelers on dusty roads... sung by mothers over cradles.
And now, sung over you.
The Lord is my shepherd.
Not a shepherd far away... but my shepherd.
The one who knows your name... who counted every hair... who sees you now, in the dark.
I shall not want.
Not because you have everything... but because the shepherd is enough.
He makes you lie down in green pastures.
Picture that soft grass, cool and deep.
The shepherd does not drive you there.
He makes you lie down... as a father lays down a tired child.
He leads you beside still waters.
Water that does not rush or churn... but rests.
The shepherd knows you cannot drink from a raging stream.
He brings you to the quiet place.
He restores your soul.
All that is scattered in you... he gathers.
All that is worn... he mends.
Breathe that in now.
Restoration... slow, and sure.
He leads you in paths of righteousness, for his name's sake.
Not because you have earned the way... but because his name is faithful.
And now, the valley.
The place we all must walk at times.
The shadow, the unknown... the fear that wakes us in the night.
Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort me.
Psalm twenty-three, verse four.
You do not walk it alone.
The shepherd is with you.
His rod and staff, the tools of his trade... they comfort you.
The rod to defend.
The staff to guide.
Even here, in the dark valley... you are tended.
He prepares a table before you, in the presence of your enemies.
A feast, even when the world presses close.
He anoints your head with oil.
The sign of blessing... of being set apart... of being loved.
Your cup runs over.
Not empty.
Not half full.
But spilling over, with goodness you did not measure out yourself.
Surely goodness and mercy shall follow you all the days of your life.
Not sometimes.
Not if you earn it.
But surely.
They follow you, like two faithful hounds at the shepherd's command.
And you will dwell in the house of the Lord forever.
This is where the path leads.
Not wandering... but home.
Let your breathing soften now.
Let the shepherd keep watch while you sleep.
He does not slumber.
He will not forget you in the night.
The Lord bless you, and keep you.
The Lord make his face shine upon you, and be gracious to you.
The Lord lift up his countenance upon you, and give you peace.
Now... and through all the night.
Goodnight.
