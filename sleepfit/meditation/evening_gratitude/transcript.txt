Let your shoulders drop.
Let your jaw soften.
You are held.
Take a slow breath in... and let it go.
Another breath... gentle, and easy.
This day is done.
Whatever it held... it is finished now.
And before you rest, you can take a moment to look back.
Not at everything.
Just at three small things.
Three gifts from this ordinary day.
Breathe slowly.
Let the first one come to mind.
Maybe it was something you tasted, or something you heard.
A kind word... a warm drink... a few minutes of quiet.
Let it rise gently to the surface.
And when it does... just say thank you.
Now, a second gift.
Something small you might have missed.
A tree you walked past.
A door that opened.
A moment when your body worked the way it was meant to.
Let it come.
And say thank you again.
One more.
A third small thing.
It does not have to be perfect, or big.
Maybe it was the fact that you made it through.
Maybe it was a breath of air, or a place to lie down.
Let it come.
And offer thanks.
Paul wrote to the church at Thessalonica, with a simple instruction.
In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.
First Thessalonians, chapter five, verse eighteen.
In every thing.
Not for every thing... but in the middle of it.
Even in the hard days, there are small mercies.
You have named three tonight.
Let them rest with you now.
Breathe slowly... and let your body grow heavy.
You are grateful... and you are loved.
May you sleep in peace.
Goodnight.
