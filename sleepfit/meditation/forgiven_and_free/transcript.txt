Let your eyes close gently.
Feel the weight of your body sinking into the bed beneath you.
Your head rests on the pillow.
Your shoulders soften.
Breathe in slowly through your nose... and out through your mouth.
Again, in through your nose... and out, letting the day fall away.
This has been a day like any other day... full of moments large and small.
Some of those moments you are proud of.
Others you wish you could take back.
Perhaps you spoke a word you regret.
Perhaps you lost patience.
Perhaps you failed to love as you meant to love.
It is easy, here in the quiet, to replay those moments.
To turn them over in your mind, examining each one... wondering what you should have done instead.
But tonight, you do not have to carry that weight.
Breathe in.
And breathe out... releasing the grip of guilt.
God does not ask you to lie awake rehearsing your failures.
He invites you to bring them to him... and leave them there.
When you confess what is true, he does not turn away.
He forgives.
And when God forgives, he does not keep a record.
He does not hold your sin at arm's length, ready to bring it back when you stumble again.
He removes it.
Breathe in deeply.
And out, slowly.
Scripture tells us just how far he removes it.
As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our transgressions from us.
Psalm one hundred three, verse twelve.
The east and the west are not two points on a map that might one day meet.
They stretch on forever... never touching.
That is the distance God places between you and your sin, when he forgives you.
Infinite separation.
Complete removal.
You cannot travel far enough to find what he has taken away.
So tonight, you do not need to search for it either.
You do not need to dig up what he has buried.
You do not need to remember what he has chosen to forget.
Let your breathing slow even more now.
In... and out.
You are forgiven.
Not because you have earned it... but because God is merciful.
Not because your sin was small... but because his love is great.
And you are free.
Free from the burden of carrying what he has already carried for you.
Free from the voice that says you must prove yourself worthy.
Free to rest in the truth that you are his... and he does not let go.
Feel your body grow heavy... peaceful... still.
There is nothing left to fix tonight.
Nothing left to solve, or carry, or defend.
Breathe in.
And out.
You are loved... forgiven... and free.
And now... you may sleep.
Goodnight.
