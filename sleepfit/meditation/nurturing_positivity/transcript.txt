Let your body settle into stillness now.
Release the weight of your shoulders... soften your jaw... and let your hands rest.
Close your eyes, if you haven't already.
Take a slow breath in through your nose... and let it go.
Again, breathe in gently... and release.
As you lie here in the quiet, your mind may still be turning over the day.
There may be worries that linger... or thoughts that circle without resolution.
Tonight, you are invited to turn your attention elsewhere.
Not to ignore what is real... but to let your mind rest on what is good.
Think for a moment of something true you know.
Perhaps it is that you are held by a love that does not fail.
Or that morning will come, bringing mercy that is new.
Now bring to mind something honest... something pure.
It might be the face of someone who has shown you kindness.
It might be a moment of beauty you noticed today... however small.
Let your thoughts move gently toward what is lovely.
Not what is broken, or bitter... but what stirs gratitude in you.
What is of good report.
Scripture offers this counsel.
Finally, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things are honest, whatsoever things are just, whatsoever things are pure, whatsoever things are lovely, whatsoever things are of good report; if there be any virtue, and if there be any praise, think on these things.
Philippians, chapter four, verse eight.
This is the work of peace.
To choose where your mind will dwell, as the day ends.
Breathe in slowly once more.
And as you breathe out... let go of what you cannot carry into sleep.
Rest now in what is true, and good.
May your sleep be sound... and may you wake to the kindness of God.
Goodnight.
