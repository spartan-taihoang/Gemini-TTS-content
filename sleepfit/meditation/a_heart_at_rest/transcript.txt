Not a soul striving, or achieving... but a soul that has stopped asking, and simply rests.
Think of a small child who has cried, and reached, and wanted.
And then, held close... the child grows quiet.
The wanting fades.
The questions stop.
There is only the warmth... the nearness... the being held.
