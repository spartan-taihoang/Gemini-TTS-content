Let your eyes close gently.
Feel the weight of your body, resting where you lie.
Your shoulders, your back, your legs... all held, and supported.
Take a slow breath in through your nose.
And let it go, softly, through your mouth.
Again, breathe in... and release.
Tonight, we rest in the image of being covered.
Of finding shelter... not by our own strength, but by turning toward the Lord.
Think for a moment of a bird, small and fragile, caught in a sudden storm.
The wind rises, the sky darkens, and the bird cannot fly against it.
But the mother bird spreads her wings wide... and beneath them, the little one finds safety.
Warmth.
Stillness.
Not because it earned that shelter... but because that is what the mother does.
The psalmist knew this image.
When everything felt uncertain... when danger pressed close... when the heart could not find peace on its own... there was a word to hold onto.
A promise that God himself would be that covering.
Breathe in now... and as you do, imagine drawing near to that shelter.
Breathe out... and let yourself rest beneath it.
You do not have to strive tonight.
You do not have to solve what is unsolved, or fix what is broken, or understand what is unclear.
You are invited simply to be still... and to let yourself be kept.
The Scripture says it this way.
He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth shall be thy shield and buckler.
Psalm ninety-one, verse four.
He shall cover you.
Not you covering yourself.
Not you earning refuge, or proving you deserve it.
But God, bending low... spreading his care over you like feathers, like a shield.
Under his wings, you may trust.
There is no storm tonight that is stronger than his keeping.
No worry that can reach beneath that cover.
No fear that can pull you from his hold.
Let your breath slow even more now.
In and out... easy, and soft.
Picture yourself small... safe... covered.
The darkness around you is not a threat... but a quiet place where you can finally let go.
His truth is your shield.
You do not need to be strong right now.
You only need to rest.
So rest now.
Let your body soften a little more.
Let your mind grow quiet.
And know that you are held... watched over... covered in the care of the one who does not sleep, who does not forget, who does not let go.
The Lord is your refuge tonight.
Under his wings, you are safe.
And in that safety... may you find deep, and gentle sleep.
Goodnight.
