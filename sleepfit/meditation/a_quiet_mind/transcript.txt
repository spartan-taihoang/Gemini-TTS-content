Find a comfortable position... and let your body settle into the bed.
Close your eyes, if you have not already.
Take a slow breath in through your nose... and let it go.
Again, breathe in gently... and release.
Tonight, we rest in the truth that God keeps our minds in peace... when we trust in him.
You may notice thoughts moving through your awareness.
Plans for tomorrow.
Something you forgot to do.
A worry that keeps returning.
These thoughts are like clouds, drifting across the sky.
You do not have to chase them, or push them away.
Simply notice them... and let them pass.
Breathe in slowly.
Breathe out.
Beneath the moving thoughts, is something steady.
Beneath the noise, is stillness.
Beneath the rush... is God.
He does not change with your circumstances.
He is the same when your mind is calm, and when it races.
You can turn your attention to him now... like turning your face toward light.
Another thought may come.
Let it drift by, like a cloud.
Breathe in.
And out.
Your mind does not have to be empty to be at peace.
It only needs to be anchored.
When a ship is anchored, the water still moves around it.
Waves rise and fall... but the ship stays.
Let your thoughts be like those waves.
They come... they go.
But you remain anchored in the presence of God.
Breathe slowly.
There is an ancient promise that speaks to this.
Listen as it is read.
Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.
Isaiah, chapter twenty-six, verse three.
Perfect peace.
Not because your mind is perfectly still... but because your mind is stayed on him.
When you notice you have wandered into worry, or planning... you have not failed.
Simply return.
Turn your attention back to God... like turning your face back to the light.
Breathe in his peace.
Breathe out what you cannot carry.
The clouds keep drifting.
That is their nature.
But the sky behind them does not move.
God is that steady sky.
Your trust in him is the anchor.
Let your breath be slow, and easy.
Rest now in his keeping.
He holds you through the night.
Your racing thoughts do not trouble him.
He is not anxious.
He is not overwhelmed.
And he keeps you in perfect peace.
Let yourself sink deeper into rest.
Breathe.
The Lord bless you, and keep you.
The Lord make his face to shine upon you, and be gracious to you.
The Lord lift up his countenance upon you... and give you peace.
Goodnight.
