He shall feed his flock like a shepherd: he shall gather the lambs with his arm, and carry them in his bosom, and shall gently lead those that are with young.
Isaiah, chapter forty, verse eleven.
Good Shepherd, thank You for gathering me close when I am weak and vulnerable.
You carry me with tenderness and lead me gently through each day.
I rest tonight in Your strong and caring arms, trusting Your watchful love over me.
Amen.
