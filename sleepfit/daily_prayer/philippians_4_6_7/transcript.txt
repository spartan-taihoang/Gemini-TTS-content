Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.
Philippians, chapter four, verses six and seven.
Heavenly Father, I bring You everything that weighs on my mind tonight.
With thanksgiving, I lay my requests before You.
Guard my heart and mind with Your peace that goes beyond understanding.
I release my worries into Your keeping and rest in Christ Jesus.
Amen.
