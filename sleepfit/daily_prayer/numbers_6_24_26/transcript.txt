The Lord bless thee, and keep thee: The Lord make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The Lord lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace.
Numbers, chapter six, verses twenty-four to twenty-six.
Father, bless me and keep me as I sleep.
Let Your face shine on me with grace.
Lift Your eyes to me and give me peace.
I rest in the blessing You speak over me tonight and always.
Amen.
